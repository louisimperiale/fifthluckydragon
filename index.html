About

Fifth Lucky Dragon

Named for a Japanese fishing vessel caught in atomic fallout. 1954. The Pacific. Nobody's fault and everybody's.

Fifth Lucky Dragon is an introspective music project from Indianapolis, Indiana. Songs about the things that linger — in the body, in the mind, in the room after everyone's left.

Six singles. More coming.
